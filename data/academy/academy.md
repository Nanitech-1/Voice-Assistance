# OneBridge Academy SaaS
OneBridge Academy is our flagship AI Career Intelligence SaaS platform designed for institutions and recruiters.

## AI Resume Analyzer & ATS Score Checker
Optimizes CVs for recruitment systems. It analyzes skills, structure, and keyword density to suggest score improvements.

## AI Mock Interview Studio
Provides real-time voice-based simulated mock interviews with instant performance feedback and scoring.

## Campus Hiring & Recruitments
Delivers automated campus placement tests, AI video proctoring, and custom screening workflows.
