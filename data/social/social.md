# OneBridge Social SaaS
OneBridge Social is our premium Social Media Management SaaS tailored for marketing agencies and brands.

## Visual Post Scheduling & Publishing
Publish content simultaneously across Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, and X using a drag-and-drop calendar.

## Agency Workspace & Collaboration
Features individual workspaces for clients, team approval workflows, and brand assets repository.

## Detailed Analytics
Tracks metrics such as reach, engagement, and audience demographics with automated white-label reports.
